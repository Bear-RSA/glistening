Changing your mind

Because you order from a distance, section 44 of ECTA lets you cancel without giving a reason within 7 days of receiving your order. Let us know by email, then send the products back unopened and sealed. You pay the cost of returning them. We refund what you paid within 30 days of your cancellation.

For hygiene reasons, products that have been opened or used can only be returned if they are faulty (see below), unless the law says otherwise.

Faulty or damaged products

Under the Consumer Protection Act, you can return a product that is faulty, damaged in transit, or not what you ordered within 6 months of delivery. We’ll repair, replace or refund it, whichever you choose, and cover reasonable return costs. Email us a photo of the problem first so we can sort it quickly.

Using our products

  • Read the label and ingredient list before use. Do a patch test on a small area first, especially if you have sensitive skin or allergies.
  • Stop using a product if it irritates your skin or scalp, and see a doctor if the irritation continues.
  • Our recommendations are general haircare tips, not medical advice.
  • Keep products out of children’s reach and away from your eyes.

What we collect

We only collect what we need to answer you and get your order to you.

  • From the order note on our website: your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and your message, which may describe your hair.
  • When you place an order: your delivery address and the details needed to confirm your payment.
  • When you message us on Instagram: whatever you choose to send us there. Instagram’s own privacy policy also applies.

We don’t ask for special personal information (like health or religious information). Please don’t include anything in your note that you don’t want us to know. Describing your hair type is fine.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your note and recommend products.
  • To take, confirm, pack and deliver your order.
  • To handle returns, questions and complaints.
  • To keep the records the law requires, such as tax records.

We use your information because you asked us to (consent), because we need it to sell and deliver to you (a contract), or because the law requires it. We will not send you marketing unless you have opted in, and you can opt out at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We only share it with service providers who help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Resend: delivers the order notes from our website to our inbox.
  • Vercel: hosts this website.
  • [Courier name]: receives your name, phone number and address to deliver your parcel.
  • [Email provider, e.g. Gmail]: where our inbox lives.

We may also share information if the law or a court requires it.

Information sent outside South Africa

Resend and Vercel store and process data on servers outside South Africa, including in the United States. Under section 72 of POPIA, we only use providers that protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA, through their data-protection terms. By sending us a note, you agree that it may be processed outside South Africa for the reasons above.

How long we keep it

We keep order notes and order details for as long as we need them to help you, plus the time the law requires for business and tax records (generally up to five years). After that we delete them. If you only sent a note and didn’t order, we delete it within [12] months.

How we keep it safe

Our website uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Only Glistening can access our inbox and records, and our accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step sign-in. If a breach ever puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it or delete it;
  • object to us using it, or withdraw your consent;
  • opt out of marketing at any time.

Email us at [privacy contact email] and we’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not happy with how we handled it, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Cookies

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and it doesn’t run analytics. Your browser may keep basic technical data, like a cached copy of the page, to load it faster.

Changes and contact

If we change this notice, we’ll update the date at the top. For any privacy question, email [privacy contact email] or write to the address above.

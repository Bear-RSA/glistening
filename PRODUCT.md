# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, run with `npm run dev` (user's choice).

## Users

People in South Africa looking for sulphate-free haircare that is affordable. They find Glistening mostly through Instagram and want to know what it is, that it is gentle on hair, that it won't cost too much, and how to order.

## Product Purpose

Glistening sells sulphate-free haircare. The promise in its own words: "sulphate free haircare doesn't have to dry your pockets out". The landing page exists so visitors can understand the offer and enquire / order.

## Positioning

Sulphate-free haircare at a price that is gentle on the wallet, run personally from Malmesbury, South Africa, with nationwide delivery.

## Operating Context

- Based in Malmesbury, South Africa; delivers nationwide.
- Orders and enquiries happen through Instagram DM (@u_r_glistening). No website shop, phone number, or WhatsApp number confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Primary visitor action: book / enquire (currently via Instagram DM).
- Undecided / unknown: product names, product range, prices, ingredients, WhatsApp/phone/email. Do not invent these.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Glistening. Instagram display name: "hey, ur glistening 🤎". Handle: @u_r_glistening.
- Voice: lowercase, warm, playful, direct ("dm", "🫶", "💌").

## Evidence on Hand

- Instagram bio (source: instagram.com/u_r_glistening, fetched 2026-10-15). Account is small (about 130 followers).
- No product photos, testimonials, prices, or reviews are available in the project. Do not fabricate them; mark any placeholder imagery or content for replacement.

## Product Principles

1. Affordable is part of the product, not a discount.
2. Gentle on hair is the core claim; keep it honest (sulphate-free).
3. Personal and local: a real small business, talk like the Instagram does.
4. Every path ends at a DM.

Scope: single-page business landing for Glistening. Mode: Persuade.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the bathroom mirror where the brand's own line, "hey, ur glistening", is written in lipstick. Refuses the beauty-category hero of a model photo beside a headline and a row of benefit cards.

OWN-WORLD: Glazed mint-green square tile wall with white grout as the ground and section surround; silvered, steam-fogged mirror glass as the main surface; cocoa-rose lipstick script for the voice (Caveat Brush); round bold grotesk (Gabarito) for set text in cocoa; butter-yellow sticky notes as the action surfaces; glass shelf as the product rail. Mint + cocoa + glass silver, no cream.

STORY: Visitor reads the lipstick note, learns it is sulphate-free haircare that won't dry their pockets, sees why sulphates dry hair, sees the shelf, learns ordering is a DM with nationwide delivery from Malmesbury, then DMs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tile wall frames a mirror filling ~85% of the viewport. On fogged glass: "hey, ur glistening" lipstick at display scale, the bio line beneath. A sticky note "dm to order" on the mirror's lower-right is the primary button. Glass shelf under the mirror edge. Nav set as small tile-plaque links top of the wall.

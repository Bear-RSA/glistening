Big foam isn’t the same as clean hair.

trust us on this one

Sulphates (you’ll see them on labels as SLS or SLES) are the strong detergents that make a shampoo lather up big. They clean, but they can also strip away the natural oils your hair needs, leaving it dry and frizzy.

Sulphate-free cleansers wash more gently, so your hair keeps its moisture. That’s all we do. And it shouldn’t cost a fortune.

  • Harsh sulphate shampoo

    • squeaky, stripped feel
    • dry ends, more frizz
    • big foam, harsh clean

  • Glistening, sulphate-free

    • gentle, everyday clean
    • keeps natural oils in
    • kind to your pocket too
